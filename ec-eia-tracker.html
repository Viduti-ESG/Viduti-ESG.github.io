Environmental Clearance Process

EC / EIA Lifecycle Tracker

See exactly where a project sits in India's Environmental Clearance process under the EIA Notification 2006 — from screening through to post-clearance compliance reporting — and what comes next. Free, no sign-up.

This is process guidance, not a substitute for an accredited EIA consultant. Under the EIA Notification 2006, only a NABET/QCI-accredited consultant may prepare an EIA report, and only MoEFCC or your state SEIAA can grant an Environmental Clearance. Category thresholds are set out in the Schedule to the Notification (as amended) — confirm your project's exact category on PARIVESH or with your accredited consultant; they are not reproduced here as fixed numbers because they vary by sector and change by amendment.
